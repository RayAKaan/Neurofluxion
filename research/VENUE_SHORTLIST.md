## Venue-selection gate

Before submitting, recheck the selected conference's live official CFP, track list, deadlines, paper length, anonymity policy, AI-use disclosure, IEEE proceedings route, registration/presentation rules, and any page charges. Confirm that the final paper fits a named track. Acceptance probability is not estimated here.
